# Smart Land — Voice-over Script (English)

> Read with a clear, professional, calm tone. Each segment matches the matching shot in
> `storyboard.json`. Total duration ~ 2:30.

**0:00–0:06 — Intro:**
Smart Land… the AI-powered digital audit platform.

**0:06–0:18 — The idea:**
Want to understand your digital presence online? Enter your website link or social
account, and we’ll analyze the real available signals and highlight your strengths and
weaknesses — with clear evidence.

**0:18–0:32 — Choosing the analysis type:**
Choose the right analysis for you: a website, or any of our supported social
platforms — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, TikTok, or YouTube.

**0:32–0:45 — Entering the URL:**
Type the link into the field. Smart Land validates it and then starts a real live
analysis over the internet.

**0:45–1:00 — Analysis steps:**
Follow each step in real time: fetching the page, SEO, performance, accessibility,
security, content and technical checks, then building your final report.

**1:00–1:18 — Smart Score & metrics:**
Here’s your result: an overall Smart Score plus a score per category. Every deduction is
tied to real, verifiable evidence.

**1:18–1:32 — Issues, recommendations & AI insights:**
Each issue shows its severity, evidence, why it matters, and a step-by-step fix — plus
smart insights to help you plan your improvements.

**1:32–1:46 — Dashboard & charts:**
Track your progress in the Dashboard: your projects, platform distribution, and your
score trends over time.

**1:46–1:56 — PDF report:**
Export your report as a professional PDF to share it or revisit your recommendations
anytime.

**1:56–2:10 — Key features:**
Plus more: AI insights, competitor comparison, our referral program, full Arabic and
English support, and a PWA that works right on your phone.

**2:10–2:30 — Closing:**
Start now — drop your link and let Smart Land give you a clear picture of your digital
presence.
